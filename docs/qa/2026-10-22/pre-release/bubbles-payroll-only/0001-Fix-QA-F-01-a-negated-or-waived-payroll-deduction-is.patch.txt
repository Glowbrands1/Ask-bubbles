Subject: [PATCH 1/5] Fix QA F-01: a negated or waived payroll deduction is
 never read as Yes
MIME-Version: 1.0
Content-Type: text/plain; charset=UTF-8
Content-Transfer-Encoding: 8bit

Root cause (src/lib/forms/payroll-deduct.ts, statedPayrollDeduct): the
negative pattern "don't/won't/not … deduct" allowed only "be" between the
negation and "deduct", so "will not payroll deduct $25" and "don't payroll
deduct $25" did not match it; no pattern read a waiver at all; the amount
patterns ("payroll deduct $25", "$25 payroll deduction") then answered Yes.

- Any negation of the deduction, with or without "payroll", is No
  ("will not / won't / don't / not going to / no longer / never / decided not
  to … deduct").
- A waived deduction is No ("$25 payroll deduction was waived", "we waived
  the payroll deduction").
- A clause about the deduction that is undecided ("maybe", "not sure",
  "thinking about", "possibly"…) states nothing, so the manager is asked.
- A clause that still states a Yes once its negation is taken out
  ("payroll deduct yes, don't deduct") contradicts itself and states nothing.
Every existing Yes and No reading is unchanged (existing suite green).

Tests: qa-w1-payroll-negation.test.ts (31: negative, positive, undecided,
contradictory, conversation-level) and qa-w1-payroll-proposal.test.ts (5,
through proposeFormForTurn turn by turn: No, asked, No -> Yes -> No). 24 of
the 36 fail on the previous code.
