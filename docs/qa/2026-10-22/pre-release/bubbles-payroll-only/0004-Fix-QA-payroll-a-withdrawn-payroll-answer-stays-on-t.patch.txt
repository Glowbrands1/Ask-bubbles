Subject: [PATCH 4/5] Fix QA payroll: a withdrawn payroll answer stays on the
 open form and is asked again

Root cause (src/lib/ai/form-proposal.ts, the open Corrective Action
continuation): a turn continued the open card on a payroll statement only when
the reader returned Yes or No. "actually we're not doing the $25 payroll
deduction" is deliberately not read either way (it is asked), so the turn left
the form for a general answer and the card it had already shown kept saying
"Is payroll deduct applicable? Yes". Present on both apps' main.

A turn about the deduction that cannot safely be read now continues the open
form (`payrollDeductUnclear`); the proposal re-reads the conversation, the
earlier Yes is withdrawn, and the card asks Yes or No.

Tests: qa-w1-payroll-proposal.test.ts, 4 cases (withdrawal after a Yes, three
wordings; then a plain No); 4 fail before. Chat and forms suites pass.
