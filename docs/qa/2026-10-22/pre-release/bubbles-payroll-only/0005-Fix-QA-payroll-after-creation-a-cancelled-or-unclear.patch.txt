Subject: [PATCH 5/5] Fix QA payroll after creation: a cancelled or unclear
 correction never leaves a Yes on the form

Root cause (src/lib/forms/chat-correction.ts, correctActiveForm): once a
Corrective Action Form existed, a chat turn changed its payroll answer only
when the reader returned Yes or No. "we cancelled the $25 payroll deduction",
"hold off on the $25 payroll deduction" and "not sure about the payroll
deduction anymore" returned neither, so the turn fell through to a new form
proposal: the saved form kept "Yes" and its Action Plan kept "$25 will be
deducted from ...'s pay." (both apps; on a finalized form the same turn
opened nothing). Reproduced end to end with synthetic data.

- A cancelled deduction is a waived one: "cancelled / cancel / called off"
  read No; a denied cancellation ("don't cancel ...") is asked, never read.
- An unclear statement about the deduction, on a form that asks the question,
  clears the saved answer (never leaves Yes), rebuilds the Action Plan without
  a deduction, and asks "Is payroll deduct applicable? Yes or No?". The next
  bare yes/no answers it. On a finalized form this happens on the new draft;
  the signed record is untouched.

Tests: qa-payroll-after-creation-e2e.test.ts (8, real create/draft/finalize/
read routes and the chat handlers in route order) and 6 reader cases in
qa-f01b-payroll-fail-safe.test.ts; 9 fail before. One earlier entry
("cancel the $25 payroll deduction", previously expected to be asked) now
expects No, with a comment. Chat and forms suites pass.
