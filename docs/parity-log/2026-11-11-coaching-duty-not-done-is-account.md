## 11 Nov 2026: a duty not done is a coaching card's account — Ask Bubbles only (COACHING_ASKS_WHAT_HAPPENED_ENABLED, default off)

QA, 11 Nov 2026: the flip blocker for ONE_FORM_EACH_PERSON and COACHING_ASKS_WHAT_HAPPENED, which ship together. With both flags on, `nov10-p7-one-form-each-named` failed. "Make me a coaching form for Tamsin, Corwin, and Ione for not closing duties correctly" read as no account, because `ai/coaching-what-happened.ts` took any "not" as "only what did not happen". So every card asked what happened and dropped the "I have the employee" line that CARD_NAMES_EMPLOYEE turns into "I have **<name>**". Now "not / never <verb>ing" ("not closing", "never following") is an account. "Not late", "nothing happened" and "not doing anything wrong" are still no account. The change sits behind COACHING_ASKS_WHAT_HAPPENED, which is off in production. New replay case `nov10-p7-one-form-each-named-with-coaching-asks` runs with all three flags.

Not needed in Ask Sunny. There, the coaching and CA "what happened" reading (`ai/ca-what-happened.ts`, CA_SALES_POLICY, on in production) already reads "a 'not' before what the employee failed to do" as the account. Only a denied fault or "nothing happened" counts as no account.
