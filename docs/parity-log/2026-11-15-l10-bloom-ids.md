## 15 Nov 2026: DM L10 Bloom-style IDS (part e)

Port from Ask Sunny #1045 (`2be8e67`). On the Bloom-style DM L10, IDS gets an owner picker on the quick-add (it starts
on the person adding), a "!" priority circle with ! issues sorted first, the owner's initials on each row (in place of
the added-by initials once an owner is set, as in the Hub) and an Oldest / Newest sort. `Ids.tsx` (`bloom` mode),
`Issue.priority` in `lib/types.ts` and the `MeetingView.tsx` props are copied as they are.

Ask Bubbles differences: none in the code. Its IDS keeps `RANKING = false`. The "!" red is a status colour, and the
accents use the bronze tokens, which Ask Bubbles' `.hub-l10` maps to its teal. The existing "Added by" test now looks
for the owner's initials, on purpose, as in Sunny.

Sibling app: already in Ask Sunny (#1045, `2be8e67`); this is its port.
