# A no-show to an opening shift is Attendance (Bubbles-only)

NO_SHOW_OPENING_IS_ATTENDANCE_ENABLED (QA's ruling on live Bubbles round 17b,
10 Nov 2026). "did not show up for a scheduled opening shift" was cited as Late
Opening here because "opening shift" is in this app's late-opening reader. The
sibling app already cites Attendance for that wording with production's flags
(checked 10 Nov 2026 through its replay pack), and has no "opening shift" in its
late-opening reader, so nothing is ported.
