# This app only: "make it about <incident> on <date>" on a saved draft is written (STEP_B_MAKE_IT_ABOUT_ACCOUNT_ENABLED)

QA's live round-6 trace (9 Nov 2026): on a saved Coaching draft about a missed meeting on 10/10/26, "make it about leaving the front desk unattended on 11/6/26" answered "I haven't changed the Coaching Form … everything on it is as it was". STEP_B_MAKE_IT_ABOUT_ACCOUNT handed the turn to the old path, and its revision's new account was refused by the reducer's protected values (`form-reducer.ts` keepsProtected), because it dropped 10/10/26, the form's Date. Now a made-about incident with its own single date stays on Step B (`form-patch-turn.ts`). The old path's revision (`reviseActiveForm`, unchanged) rewrites the account first, and its date check follows the incident's own date (`form-write.ts`, `made-about-date.ts`). Only then are that date and the reader's edits (Other, the topic) written. The reply comes from the whole diff. Following QA's ruling, an account that is not rewritten changes nothing, because the Date, Other and the topic describe the same incident: the reply says so and asks what the account should say.

Not needed in the sibling app: it has no STEP_B_MAKE_IT_ABOUT_ACCOUNT hand-off (`form-patch-turn.ts` on its main 24a380e). There, a "make it about …" turn on a saved draft writes the reader's edits on Step B and never reaches the revision. That is the gap this app's flag closed in round 5; porting the flag itself is a separate item.
